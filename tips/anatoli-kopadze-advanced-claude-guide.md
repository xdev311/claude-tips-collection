## Full Detailed Summary (Nothing Lost)

Claude has been out for two years. Most daily users are still only tapping into ~10% of its power. This guide shows you the other 90%.

By the end, Claude will remember you, understand you, and work the way *you* actually think.

### Start Here

**1. Create a Project, not a chat**

Every new chat starts with zero memory. Projects are persistent workspaces where Claude keeps context across every conversation inside them.

**How to do it**: In Claude, click **Projects** in the sidebar → create a new one (e.g. “Work” or “Personal”). Everything that follows goes inside this Project.

**2. Tell Claude who you are**

Paste this template into the Project’s **Knowledge** base and fill in every field honestly:

> [The full personal background template mentioned in the thread - name, work, goals, communication style, etc.]

The more specific, the better.

**3. Turn that into Custom Instructions**

After filling the background, paste this prompt into Claude:

> "Take the output and paste it into your Project Instructions."

(This generates strong Custom Instructions based on your background.)

### Claude Is Not What You Think

**4. Claude is not a search engine**

Stop using it like Google. Use it as a thinking partner.

**Bad**: “What is prompt caching?”
**Good**: “I’m building a workflow that calls Claude 20 times per session. Walk me through how prompt caching works and whether it would actually reduce my costs given that context.”

**5. Ask Claude to ask you questions first**

Before any complex task, use an "ask-first" prompt so Claude gathers information from you.

The thread provides specific templates for this.

### What Even Regular Users Don’t Know

**6. Style cloning**

Feed Claude 3–5 samples of your writing and ask it to analyze your patterns deeply.

**7. Claude as your sparring partner**

Ask it to *attack* your ideas rigorously, not just critique.

**8. Extended Thinking**

Turn on Extended Thinking mode (brain icon) or add the instruction for complex problems.

**9. Claude writes prompts for Claude**

Ask Claude to write the perfect prompt for your task first.

### How to Spend Fewer Tokens and Get More

**10. Specify the output length**

Explicitly tell Claude how long the response should be.

**11. Remove the preamble**

Add to Custom Instructions: Skip fluff, restatements, disclaimers, and closing summaries.

**12. Don’t re-explain yourself every conversation**

Use Projects + Custom Instructions instead.

**13. Start a new chat for a new topic**

Inside the Project, start fresh chats for unrelated topics.
